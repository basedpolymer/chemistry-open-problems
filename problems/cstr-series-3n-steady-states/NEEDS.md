# Paywalled sources (useful for the priority check; the proofs do not depend on them)

- A. Varma, Ind. Eng. Chem. Fundam. 19 (1980) 316–319, doi:10.1021/i160075a016. Confirm which model gives the
  bound 2^(n+1) − 1 (adiabatic / isothermal, one state variable per reactor) and that it makes no claim about
  jacketed reactors.
- R.-S. Li, Chem. Eng. Sci. 49 (1994) 3053–3057, doi:10.1016/0009-2509(94)E0091-4. The abstract extends the count
  to 3^N states, 2^N stable, for N isothermal CSTRs. Check whether this is proved, and confirm that it only covers
  isothermal kinetics.
- M. Kubíček, H. Hofmann, V. Hlaváček, J. Sinkule, Chem. Eng. Sci. 35 (1980) 987–996,
  doi:10.1016/0009-2509(80)85088-3. Check for any statement about N nonadiabatic reactors.
- D. G. Retzloff, P. C.-H. Chan, C. C. Chicone, I. J. Papick, SIAM J. Appl. Math. 52 (1992) 1136–1147,
  doi:10.1137/0152067. Check for any n-reactor statement beyond the abstract.

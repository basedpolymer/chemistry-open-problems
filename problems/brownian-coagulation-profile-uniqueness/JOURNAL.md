## First candidate rejected after novelty check

Nguyen–Tang's catalytic reaction–diffusion conjecture, [DOI 10.1007/s00033-026-02847-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00033-026-02847-0), initially looked promising. The published nonnegative-data wording admits the stationary catalyst-free boundary state, so the intended positive-data question must be distinguished from that literal counterexample.

Further checks found [AIM entry 416](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/8729eab98bafe409b66c5e39253bf56569a62327/problems/416-catalytic-reaction-diffusion-positive-attractor.md), already marked solved following a 15 October review, with a public manuscript for the positive-data question. That manuscript was not re-proved here. This is enough reason not to propose rediscovering the same result as the next open target.

## Selected target

Brownian Smoluchowski finite-mass similarity-profile uniqueness. Confirmed the original open statement and checked the assumptions of the 2017 and 2021 perturbative results. Examined a 2024 citing paper whose uniqueness conclusion might initially look broader; its constant-along-rays hypothesis excludes the Brownian kernel.

The selection audit found no later resolution. Searches have the access and indexing limitations recorded in `references.md`. The source PDFs are already available locally, so obtaining articles is not a blocker.

The proposed route combines global a priori bounds with analysis of the linearized profile equation. Continuing the known constant-kernel branch is only a possible component: exclusion of all other admissible profiles remains the crucial proof obligation.

**State at handoff:** problem selected and precisely documented; mathematical resolution not attempted or claimed in this scouting turn.

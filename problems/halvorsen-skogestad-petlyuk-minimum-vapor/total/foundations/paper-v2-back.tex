(written separately by Agents F, S, N and P from the equations of Definition~\ref{def:A}) check the identities and the
bound on finite-stage steady states; paths are relative to the problem folder, and the environment is WSL Fedora
with Python~3, numpy, scipy, sympy, mpmath, pyomo and ipopt.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{total/network/scripts/unit\_identities\_exact.py} (exact rationals and sympy): Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform}
symbolically for $N=2,\dots,6$, and the summed node balance (the cut identity for an arbitrary set of units) on random
unit graphs with recycles, arbitrary subsets and rational $\theta$. PASS.
\item \texttt{total/network/scripts/section\_identity\_exact.py} (exact rationals): the identity
$\Lambda_s(\theta)=L\,G_{x_{k+1}}(\theta)-(\theta V/S(x_k))G_{x_k}(\theta)$ and the single change of side per section, on 400
random stage-to-stage profiles with net flows of both signs. PASS.
\item \texttt{total/foundations/scripts/unitnet.py} (Agent F; floating point, Newton with analytic Jacobian): a binary
column above and below its minimum vapor (below it the products are impure and the bound is attained to $10^{-11}$

\item \texttt{total/network/scripts/cut\_bound\_check.py} (Agent N): random section/junction networks and a ternary
Petlyuk grid with 8--40 stages per section. PASS. \texttt{permissive\_sign\_path.py}: the light-to-heavy sign path of the

\item \texttt{total/simulation/scripts/test\_stage\_bound.py} (Agent P, own solver with pseudo-transient continuation

\section{Scope, limitations and open questions}\label{sec:scope2}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{What is proved.} The lower bound $R\ge\sum_P\max(0,\varphi_P(\theta_j))$, hence $R^*(\mathcal S)\ge\max_jH_j$, for every
arrangement of $\mathcal A$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:stage}, Corollary~\ref{cor:purity}); all statements of version~1 in the
Underwood model (Sections~\ref{sec:setting}--\ref{sec:achieve}), whose verification is in \texttt{verification.md}.
\item \emph{Attainment.} The equality $R^*(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{Petlyuk}})=\max_jH_j$, which implies the conjecture once the lower
bound is known, is proved in Underwood's infinite-stage model for every $N$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:achieve}) and in the
finite-stage model for $N=2$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:binary}). For $N\ge3$, steady states exist for all prescribed flows
(Proposition~\ref{prop:exist}), an isolated prefractionator admits exact finite-stage profiles with exponentially small
traces, and the full network reaches purities tending to $1$ at $R=\max_jH_j+\delta$ in three independent
simulations; a proof for the closed network is not given (Section~\ref{sec:attain} describes the reduction for $N=3$).
\item \emph{Class.} $\mathcal A$ contains every arrangement within the assumptions of~\autocite{HS3}; the devices it excludes
(heat exchange between process streams not counted in $R$, several pressures, non-ideal mixtures, decanters,
membranes, component-dependent efficiencies, heat pumps, several feeds) are excluded by the conjecture itself. The
bound concerns the total vaporization; with heat exchange between process streams the external heat can be lower
(Section~\ref{sec:conseq}), and its minimum is not determined here.
\item \emph{Extensions.} For several feeds the same argument gives
\[R\ge\sup_\theta\Bigl[\sum_P\max(0,\varphi_P(\theta))-\sum_kf_k\max(0,\varphi_{F_k}(\theta))\Bigr],\]
and for non-sharp product specifications $R\ge\max_j\sum_P\max(0,\varphi_P(\theta_j))$ as it
stands (\texttt{total/network/network.md}, \S7); whether these bounds are attained is not studied. Constant molar overflow enters only
through the energy balance: under the constant-heat-transport model of~\autocite{MTA2023} (constant but
component-dependent molar latent heats $\lambda_i$, no sensible heat), the same proof applies verbatim to the
latent-heat-weighted flows $\tilde n_{e,i}=\lambda_in_{e,i}$, because the weighted mole fractions satisfy
$\tilde y_i=\alpha_i\tilde x_i/\sum_k\alpha_k\tilde x_k$ again, and bounds the total heat by the highest peak of the
correspondingly weighted $V_{\min}$ diagram. Non-ideal mixtures are open: sign uniformity uses constant relative
volatilities.
\item \emph{Version~1's permissive model.} The bound fails there for $N=5$ (Section~\ref{sec:cex}); version~2 shows that
this point is not a limit of physical operations, so the 19 undecided permissive pairs no longer bear on the
conjecture.
\end{enumerate}

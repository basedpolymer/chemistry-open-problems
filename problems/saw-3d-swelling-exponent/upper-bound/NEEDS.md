# Upper-bound solver: sources needed

Supplied and used (thanks): Kesten 1963, 1964; Madras–Slade 2013 reprint excerpts (Ch. 3, Ch. 4, extras);
Chayes–Chayes 1986; Hammersley–Welsh 1962; Madras 1988; Madras 2014.

Still useful (paywalled), lower priority:
- Duminil-Copin, Hammond, Comm. Math. Phys. 324 (2013) 401-423, doi:10.1007/s00220-013-1811-1 (published version):
  to check that theorem/lemma numbers (Lemma 2.2, Thm 2.3, Cor. 2.4, Thm 2.5, Lemma 3.9, eq. (4.2)) agree with
  arXiv:1205.0401v1, which is what I cite.
- Krachun, Panagiotis, Ann. Probab. (2026), doi:10.1214/24-AOP1730 (published version): to check that Lemma 2.2,
  Lemma 2.3, Lemma 4.1, Theorem 1, Theorem 2 and the quoted sentences of p. 2-3 agree with arXiv:2310.17299v1.
- N. Madras, "Bounds on the critical exponent of self-avoiding polygons", in Random Walks, Brownian Motion and
  Interacting Particle Systems (Progr. Probab. 28, 1991), doi:10.1007/978-1-4612-0459-6_19 (if that is the right
  DOI): polygon generating-function bounds in d = 3, relevant to attempts.md D5.

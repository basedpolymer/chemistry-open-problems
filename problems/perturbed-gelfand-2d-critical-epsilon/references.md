## Phase 2: proof sources and renewed access attempts

Read in detail the [author-hosted published-version manuscript](https://homepages.uc.edu/~kormanp/KLO-2017.pdf), dated 27 June 2017: Section 3, including the test-function proof of Theorem 3.1; Section 4, the Du–Lou transformation, Theorem 4.1 and Lemma 4.1; Section 5, the small-parameter compactness proof and ordering argument; and Section 6. The global proof here invokes the exact qualitative conclusions of Theorems 3.1 and 4.1, after checking their hypotheses for `g(q)=exp(-1/q)`. It replaces the unspecified small-parameter compactness step by a stopping-level graph and a finite interval cover. This is our deduction, not a result claimed by that source.

The author's 2017 version prints approximate ε₀ `0.2385` on printed page 3 and approximate limiting centre `1.53` in Section 6. These numerical approximations are not used as proof inputs. Our independent floating-point exploration and rigorous cusp enclosure give ε₀ near `0.24210616559525`; the certified limiting-centre bracket `(1.5,1.6)` is compatible with the second approximation.
